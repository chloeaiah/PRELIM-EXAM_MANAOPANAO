## 🌸 01 | Activity Description

For this activity, I explored how **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** can assist in creating a professional resume using two Large Language Models (LLMs): **ChatGPT and Claude AI**.

I provided both AI tools with the same prompt and personal information to generate a one-page resume for a third-year Civil Engineering student applying for an internship or On-the-Job Training (OJT) position.

The main objective was to compare their outputs based on structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality. This allowed me to observe how different AI platforms organize and present the same academic background, technical skills, and project experience.

## 🧁 02 | What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned that AI tools can make creating professional documents easier, faster, and more organized.

Both ChatGPT and Claude AI transformed the information I provided into professional resume formats, but they presented the information differently.

**My key takeaways:**

- 💗 AI can help create and organize professional documents.
- 💜 Clear and detailed prompts can improve AI-generated outputs.
- 🌷 Different AI tools can interpret the same instructions in different ways.
- 🎀 AI-generated information should always be reviewed for accuracy.
- ✨ A professional resume should reflect actual skills, qualifications, and experiences.

---

## 🤖 03 | Comparing ChatGPT & Claude AI

I used the same prompt for both platforms to make the comparison fair and consistent.

| Criteria | ChatGPT | Claude AI |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Structure** | Organized into Professional Summary, Education, Technical Skills, Academic Project, Additional Qualifications, and Certifications. | Organized into Profile, Education, Technical Skills, Academic Project, and Certifications. |
| **Clarity** | Uses direct and concise wording to describe academic background and qualifications. | Uses descriptive bullet points to explain technical skills and academic project details. |
| **Formatting** | Uses clear headings and compact bullet points. | Uses a minimalist layout with section dividers and grouped information. |
| **Overall Quality** | Provides a useful and organized starting draft for an internship application. | Provides a structured resume with a clear presentation of academic and technical qualifications. |

*Both outputs still require personal review and verification before actual submission.*

## 💗 04 | Challenges & Solutions

One of the challenges I encountered was comparing the two AI-generated resumes while ensuring that both were evaluated fairly.

Since both platforms produced different layouts and writing styles, I needed to examine their strengths based on the same criteria.

**How I addressed these challenges:**

- 🎀 Used the exact same prompt for both AI platforms.
- 🌸 Compared their section organization and readability.
- 💜 Examined how each tool presented my technical skills and academic project.
- ✨ Checked that the generated information remained accurate and did not exaggerate my qualifications.

This helped me understand that AI-generated content should not automatically be accepted without proper evaluation.

## 🎨 06 | Creative Process & Design Choices

The main focus of this activity was to explore how two AI platforms interpret identical instructions and transform information into professional resume formats.

I chose a **clean, modern, and minimalist resume style** because professional documents should be easy to read and organized.

**My design observations:**

- 🌷 **ChatGPT:** Used separate headings and concise bullet points to organize the information.
- 🪻 **Claude AI:** Used a structured layout with clear section dividers, grouped technical skills, and additional project descriptions.
- 🎀 **Consistency:** Both resumes followed a professional one-page format suitable for an entry-level internship application.

The comparison helped me recognize how visual hierarchy, spacing, and content organization contribute to a resume's overall presentation.

## 💭 07 | Personal Reflection

This activity allowed me to explore the practical applications of Artificial Intelligence in preparing professional documents. By comparing ChatGPT and Claude AI, I realized that different AI tools can produce different approaches even when given the exact same prompt.

I learned that the quality of an AI-generated resume depends not only on the platform but also on the accuracy of the information and clarity of the instructions provided.

Most importantly, I realized that AI should serve as an assisting tool rather than a replacement for personal judgment. A resume should always be reviewed, edited, and personalized to represent the applicant's real qualifications and experiences.
