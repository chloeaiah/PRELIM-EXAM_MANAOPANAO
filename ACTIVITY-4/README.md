## 🔵 01 — 📌 ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

This activity involved a group presentation about **how to use Google Drive**. The main objective was to introduce Google Drive as a digital platform for storing, organizing, accessing, and sharing files online.

Through our presentation, we discussed how Google Drive helps students manage academic files, collaborate with classmates, and access important documents using different devices. The activity demonstrated the importance of digital tools in making schoolwork and group activities more organized and convenient.

## 🟢 02 — 🌸 WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

Through this activity, I learned more about the features and practical uses of Google Drive, especially how it improves file management and collaboration.

Some of the key things I learned include:

- **Cloud Storage:** Understanding how files can be stored online and accessed whenever needed.
- **File Organization:** Learning how to create folders and organize documents for easier access.
- **File Sharing:** Understanding how to share files and folders through links and access permissions.
- **Collaboration:** Recognizing how Google Drive supports group activities and allows people to work together efficiently.

I also learned the importance of teamwork, communication, and proper preparation when presenting information to an audience.

## 🟡 03 — 🧩 CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

One of the challenges we encountered was organizing the presentation content and making sure that the information about Google Drive was explained clearly and understandably.

Since it was a group presentation, we also needed to coordinate with one another, divide the topics, and ensure that our individual parts connected smoothly.

We addressed these challenges by dividing the presentation responsibilities, organizing the important information, and coordinating with our group members. This helped us prepare a more structured presentation and communicate the topic effectively.

## 🔴 04 — 🚀 FUTURE APPLICATIONS

The knowledge I gained from this activity can be applied to future academic activities, particularly when handling group projects, research papers, presentations, and other engineering-related requirements.

As a Civil Engineering student, I can use Google Drive to organize project documents, store important files, share academic resources, and collaborate with classmates. These skills can also be useful in professional settings where teamwork, file management, and document sharing are essential.

## 🔵 05 — 🎨 CREATIVE PROCESS & PRESENTATION APPROACH

For this activity, our group focused on presenting Google Drive in a simple and understandable way. We organized the presentation around the platform's purpose, features, and practical applications so that the audience could understand how it can be used in everyday academic activities.

We aimed to make the discussion informative and accessible by presenting the topic in an organized sequence. Through this process, we recognized that an effective presentation requires clear explanations, proper organization of information, teamwork, and coordination among group members.
